Cookies are small text files a website stores on your computer, phone or tablet. The rules also cover similar technologies, such as the browser's *local storage*. This page lists what mors.coffee stores, and why.

## Our own statistics set no cookies

We measure traffic with Umami, which sets no cookies and does not recognise you across websites. Read more in our [privacy policy](/en/privacy-policy).

## What we store in your browser

| Name | Type | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| `mors:stampcard-teaser-dismissed` | Local storage | Remembers that you closed the stamp card box, so it does not show again | Until you delete it |

This stays in your browser and is never sent to us.

## Third parties: Google Maps

The front page and contact page show a map from Google Maps. When the map loads, Google may set its own cookies and receive information about your visit, including your IP address. We have no control over these cookies. Read more in [Google's cookie policy](https://policies.google.com/technologies/cookies).

## How to delete cookies

You can always delete cookies and local storage in your browser settings. See the guide for your browser:

- [Google Chrome](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/95647)
- [Safari](https://support.apple.com/guide/safari/sfri11471/mac)
- [Firefox](https://support.mozilla.org/kb/clear-cookies-and-site-data-firefox)
- [Microsoft Edge](https://support.microsoft.com/microsoft-edge)
