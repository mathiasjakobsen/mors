## When you visit the website

**Statistics.** We use [Umami](https://umami.is) to see how the website is used – for example which pages are visited, where visitors come from, and what type of device and browser they use. Umami sets no cookies. We also record how the pages are used (for example clicks and scrolling) so we can improve them. Umami runs on a server operated in the EU by our supplier (Not Rocket Science), and the data is not shared with anyone else.\
*Purpose and legal basis:* improving the website, GDPR Art. 6(1)(f) (legitimate interest).

**Hosting.** The website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc.). Like any web server, GitHub logs technically necessary data such as your IP address when you visit. GitHub is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.\
*Legal basis:* Art. 6(1)(f) (legitimate interest in running a secure website).

**Map.** The front page and contact page show a map from Google Maps. When the map loads, Google receives information about your visit, including your IP address, and may set cookies. Read more in our [cookie policy](/en/cookie-policy) and in [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Stamp card

When you get a stamp card, we register you as a member with the e-mail address or phone number you enter. We record your stamps and when you last used the card. The card lives in Apple Wallet or Google Wallet, and when you add it, Apple or Google process information about the card under their own terms.\
*Purpose and legal basis:* providing the stamp card you asked for, Art. 6(1)(b).

We keep the data for as long as you use the card. Write to us if you want it deleted.

## Purchases in the coffee bar

When you pay by card, the payment is processed by our payment provider, SumUp. We never see your full card details. We keep receipts and accounting records for 5 years, as Danish bookkeeping law requires.\
*Legal basis:* Art. 6(1)(b) and (c).

## When you write to us

If you contact us by e-mail or phone, we use the information to reply. We delete the correspondence when it is no longer relevant.\
*Legal basis:* Art. 6(1)(f).

**Job applications.** If you send an application to [job@mors.coffee](mailto:job@mors.coffee), we only use it to consider you for a position. If you do not get the job, we delete the application no later than 6 months afterwards, unless you allow us to keep it longer.\
*Legal basis:* Art. 6(1)(b) and (f).

## Who do we share data with?

We never sell your data. We use suppliers (data processors) for hosting, statistics, e-mail, our point-of-sale system and payments, and they may only process the data on our instructions. If data is transferred to countries outside the EU/EEA, this happens on a lawful basis, such as the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

## Your rights

You have the right to:

- access the data we process about you
- have incorrect data corrected
- have data erased
- have processing restricted
- object to processing based on legitimate interest
- receive your data in a common format (data portability)

Write to [hej@mors.coffee](mailto:hej@mors.coffee) to use your rights.

## Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency, **Datatilsynet**, Carl Jacobsens Vej 35, 2500 Valby, Denmark – [datatilsynet.dk](https://www.datatilsynet.dk).
